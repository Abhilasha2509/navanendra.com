About

Industry Experience
for Real-World Impact

I am an Assistant Professor at NIFT Patna with over 15 years of professional experience spanning apparel manufacturing and academia. With more than 13 years of experience in the apparel industry across production, quality and operations, my journey from industry to academia allows me to bring real-world manufacturing insights into teaching, research and industry engagement.

As the Regional Industry Coordinator (RIC) at NIFT Patna, I work towards strengthening industry–academia collaboration and creating meaningful opportunities for students, faculty and the apparel sector.
