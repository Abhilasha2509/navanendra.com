About

Industry Experience
for Real-World Impact

I am an Assistant Professor at NIFT Patna with over 15 years of experience in apparel manufacturing, production, quality and operations. My journey from industry to academia allows me to bring real-world manufacturing insights into teaching, research and industry engagement.

As the Regional Industry Coordinator (RIC) at NIFT Patna, I work towards strengthening industry–academia collaboration and creating meaningful opportunities for students, faculty and the apparel sector.
